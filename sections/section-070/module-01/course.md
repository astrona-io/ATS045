# Admission Policy And Extension Trust

This module develops the reasoning needed to distinguish mutation, validation and authorization.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish mutation, validation and authorization.
- Explain built-in, declarative and webhook admission mechanisms.
- Relate failure policy and exemptions to effective enforcement.
- Identify extension credentials, transport and availability requirements.

## Before You Start

Read the examples as descriptions of system behavior. No cluster, account, laboratory or installed tool is required. Technical terms are defined where they are used.
