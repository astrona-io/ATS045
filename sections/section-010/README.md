# Supply Chain Security

This section teaches supply chain trust and verification. These ideas help you explain system behavior and choose a suitable approach in a KCSA scenario.

You will work through Supply Chain Trust And Verification. Each module introduces its goals, teaches the concepts through examples, and closes with a summary. Reusable YAML checkpoints review related objectives after selected section summaries. The course assessment index records their placement and coverage.

## Learning Outcomes

- Trace source, build, publication and deployment trust boundaries.
- Distinguish provenance, signatures, inventories and vulnerability findings.
- Relate admission decisions to exact artifact identity.
- Explain continued maintenance after an artifact is approved.
